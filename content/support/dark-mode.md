## Getting Started

### Turning On Dark Mode in Safari

Dark Mode works inside Safari, so Safari needs to be allowed to use it. The **Safari Setup Guide** in the app walks you through these steps.

1. Open **Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions**. On iOS 17, it's **Settings › Safari › Extensions**.
2. Tap **Dark Mode** and turn on **Allow Extension**.
3. Under **Permissions**, set **All Websites** to **Allow**, so Dark Mode can reach every page.

You can also do this from Safari itself: tap the puzzle icon in the address bar, then **Manage Extensions**.

### Opening Dark Mode in Safari

On any website, tap the puzzle icon in Safari's address bar, then **Dark Mode**. This opens Safari's Dark Mode menu, where you can change the theme for every website or just the one you're on. Settings you change there show up in the app too.

## On, Auto and Off

- **On** makes every website dark.
- **Auto** turns dark mode on following a schedule.
- **Off** leaves websites as they are.

### Choosing What Auto Follows

In the app, open **Schedule**:

- **System appearance** follows your iPhone or iPad's own light and dark appearance.
- **Sunset to sunrise** turns dark mode on after sunset. If you allow location, sunset is worked out from where you are, rounded to about a kilometre. If you don't, it's estimated from your time zone, which is usually within an hour of the real time.
- **Custom schedule** turns dark mode on and off at times you choose.

## Themes

Open **Theme** in the app to pick one of four presets: **Default**, **Midnight**, **Warm** and **OLED** (pure black). The sliders below them change brightness, contrast, sepia and grayscale. The preview at the top shows a page in your theme as you change it.

Safari's Dark Mode menu has more options, such as the background and text colors, the font, and the Dynamic or Filter mode.

## Changing One Website

In Safari's Dark Mode menu, while you're on the website:

- **Turn off the switch** next to the website's name to leave that site as it is.
- Choose **Apply to › This website**, then change the theme. Your changes stay on that site only.

The **Websites** screen in the app lists every website you've changed. Swipe left on one, or tap **Edit**, to remove it.

### Websites That Already Have a Dark Theme

Dark Mode leaves websites that already have their own dark theme as they are. To use Dark Mode's theme on one instead, open Safari's Dark Mode menu on that site and turn it on.

## Syncing and Backups

The app and Safari's Dark Mode menu share one set of settings: a change in either shows in the other.

Turn on **Sync with iCloud** in the app to use the same settings on every iPhone and iPad signed in to your Apple Account.

**Backup & Restore** in the app exports your settings to a file and imports them again. Importing a backup replaces your current settings. **Reset All Settings** puts everything back the way it was when you installed the app.

## Troubleshooting

### Websites Aren't Turning Dark

- Check that Dark Mode is set to **On**, or to **Auto** during its scheduled hours.
- Check that the extension is turned on and allowed on **All Websites** (see [Getting Started](#getting-started)).
- Check that the website isn't switched off in Safari's Dark Mode menu.
- Reload the page.

### A Page Looks Wrong

Some pages draw in ways the default Dynamic mode can't read. In Safari's Dark Mode menu, choose **Apply to › This website**, then set **Mode** to **Filter**.

Fixes for many websites arrive with the site rules, which update by themselves about once a day. To check for new ones now, open Safari's Dark Mode menu, then **Settings › Site rules › Update now**.

### Dark Mode Isn't in Safari's Menu

Turn the extension on in **Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions**. If it's already on, turn it off and on again, then reload the page.

### My Settings Don't Match on My Other Device

Make sure **Sync with iCloud** is on in the app on both devices, and that both are signed in to the same Apple Account. Changes can take a little while to arrive.

### Sunset to Sunrise Isn't Using My Location

Dark Mode asks for location only once. If you chose not to allow it, sunset is estimated from your time zone. To allow it later, open **Settings › Apps › Dark Mode › Location** and choose **While Using the App**.
